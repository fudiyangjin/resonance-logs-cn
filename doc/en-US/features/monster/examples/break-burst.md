# Time your burst to the break and HP thresholds

Related page: [Stun, HP and Teammate Fantasies](../status.md)

**Scenario**: When the Boss's stun bar breaks, it takes extra damage for a while, so you want to see how much stun is left and save your burst for the break. The Boss also changes phase at a certain HP percentage, and you want to time your skills to it.

## Step 1: Turn on the stun and HP bars

1. **{{ui:routes.tools.monsterMonitor}} → {{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.overlay}}**, turn on the **Stun Bar Area** and the **HP Bar Area** (both off by default).
2. Click **{{ui:monsterMonitor.actions.editLayout}}** and stack them at the top center of the screen, stun above HP.

## Step 2: Adjust the style

- In **{{ui:monsterMonitor.stun.styleTitle}}** under **{{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.stun}}**, give the stun bar a color that is easy to tell apart from the HP bar and the same width;
- In **{{ui:monsterMonitor.hp.styleTitle}}** under **{{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.hp}}**, raise the font size so the percentage is easier to read.

## Step 3 (optional): Teammates' fantasies

Open **{{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.fantasy}}** and turn on **{{ui:monsterMonitor.fantasy.showAll}}**, or search only for the fantasies you care about; then turn on the **Teammate Fantasy Area** in Enable Window.

## Result

Get your burst ready as the stun bar nears 0 and go all out the moment it breaks; as the HP nears the phase-change percentage, use your skills as planned. Both bars follow your current target.
