# Threat List

**{{ui:routes.tools.monsterMonitor}} → {{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.hate}}** shows the threat ranking of the current target. Tanks can confirm they are holding threat, and DPS can avoid pulling aggro.

## Settings

- **{{ui:monsterMonitor.hate.maxDisplay}}**: 5–20 players;
- **{{ui:monsterMonitor.hate.styleTitle}}**: spacing, font size, colors, progress bar.

> [!NOTE]
> **Hate List Area** is off by default; turn it on in [Enable Window](./overlay.md).
