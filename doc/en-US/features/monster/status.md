# Stun, HP and Teammate Fantasies

These three tabs correspond to three areas of the monster overlay, and all of them follow the current target.

## Stun bar

**{{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.stun}}** shows the target's current stun value / maximum stun value; when it reaches 0 the target is broken. Useful for the party to burst together during the break window.

**{{ui:monsterMonitor.stun.styleTitle}}** lets you adjust colors and size.

## HP bar

**{{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.hp}}** shows the target's HP and percentage, useful for timing phase transitions or execute thresholds.

**{{ui:monsterMonitor.hp.styleTitle}}** lets you adjust colors and size.

## Teammate fantasies

**{{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.fantasy}}** shows the resonance fantasies summoned by your teammates.

- Turn on **{{ui:monsterMonitor.fantasy.showAll}}** to list every teammate fantasy;
- Or search by name / MonsterId to show only the few you care about.

**{{ui:monsterMonitor.fantasy.styleTitle}}** lets you adjust row spacing, font size and colors.

> [!NOTE]
> **Stun Bar Area** and **HP Bar Area** are off by default; turn them on in [Enable Window](./overlay.md).
