# Teammate Buffs

**{{ui:routes.tools.monsterMonitor}} → {{ui:monsterMonitor.tabs.teammate}}** shows, as a matrix, which Buffs **each teammate** has applied to the current target. Useful for shot-callers and supports who want to confirm the whole party's Debuffs are in place.

## Settings

- Search for Buffs to add them to **{{ui:monsterMonitor.teammate.groupTitle}}**, or add them quickly by category;
- **{{ui:monsterMonitor.teammate.styleTitle}}**: **{{ui:monsterMonitor.style.rowHeight}}**, **{{ui:monsterMonitor.style.nameColumnWidth}}**, **{{ui:monsterMonitor.style.buffColumnWidth}}** and colors.

Whether it appears in the overlay is controlled by **Teammate Buff Area** in [Enable Window](./overlay.md).
